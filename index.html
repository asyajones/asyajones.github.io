Asya Jones

I build pathways between learning and what comes next.

I’m a learning strategist, educator, and researcher working at the intersection of learning, data & AI, and workforce development.

I design learning experiences and experiential pathways that help people build skills, understand where they can go next, and make successful transitions into new roles and opportunities.

What I build

Learning Experiences

I design learning that moves beyond content delivery toward practice, judgment, communication, and transfer.

Experiential Pathways

I connect learning to opportunity through internships, practicums, career exploration, and other experiences that help people navigate transitions.

Data & AI Enablement

I help people develop the skills and confidence to work with data and emerging technologies—and think critically about when and how to use them.

The question behind my work

What do people need to be capable of doing next?

I use that question to work backward: from the transition someone is trying to make to the skills, experiences, evidence, and support that can help them get there.

Currently

I am a PhD candidate at the Pardee RAND Graduate School studying how Black women pursue and stack non-degree credentials as they build careers in data and data science.

I also teach graduate and professional biostatistics and statistics courses and lead Rose Data Studio, where I develop learning experiences that make quantitative methods more useful, applied, and human.
